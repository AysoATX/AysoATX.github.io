# McpHealth

McpHealth is a personal, single-user tool built and used only by its developer. It reads the developer's own Google Health (Fitbit) data, such as steps, heart rate, sleep, weight and workouts, so the developer can review it with an AI assistant.

## Privacy Policy

- The app runs on the developer's own computer. Access tokens stay on that computer and are never shared.
- The data is not sold, used for advertising, or given to anyone else. It is shown only to the AI assistant the developer uses, when the developer asks for it.
- Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account security settings.
